## How the result is judged

The result is the orchestrator's last `REPORT`. At the end of the run it is checked: every
figure cites a footnote, every footnote is a source with an exact quote or a calculation, and
a grader scores it from 1 to 5 on:

- **coverage**: it answers every question the goal asks;
- **support**: each figure is backed by the source its footnote cites, and calculations are right;
- **sources**: primary and current sources are preferred, and the date of each figure is clear;
- **conflicts**: where sources disagree, the report shows and explains it instead of averaging;
- **usefulness**: the summary gives its reader a clear answer and the trade-offs behind it.

## Rules

1. There is no human to ask during this run. Settle open questions from the goal, decide, and
   go on.
2. Never invent a source, a quote or a number. A gap you could not fill is a finding: say so.
3. Prefer primary sources: vendor pages, papers, official statistics and benchmarks, filings.
   Say how old each figure is. When sources disagree, show both and explain the difference;
   never average them silently.
4. The network is open. Fetch what you need; do not install software you do not need.
