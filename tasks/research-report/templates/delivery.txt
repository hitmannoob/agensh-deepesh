## The report

The report is markdown, posted as the detail of a `REPORT` entry:

- It starts with a `# ` title, then a summary that answers the goal directly, then one `## `
  section per question, and ends with the gaps and the limits of the sources.
- Every figure in a paragraph, list item or table row cites a footnote, `[^1]`. Footnotes are
  defined at the end of the report, each cited at least once, and are either a source copied
  from a `FACT`'s detail:

      [^1]: https://lambda.ai/pricing — "H100 SXM $2.99 / GPU / hr" (accessed 2026-11-20)

  or a calculation citing other footnotes:

      [^c1]: calc — $2.99/hr [^1] × 8,760 h = $26,192 per GPU-year

- It uses only findings on the board. A key claim that was not verified is said to be
  unverified. No placeholders (TODO, TBD).
- The board checks a `REPORT` when you post it and rejects it with the problems it found: an
  uncited figure, a footnote without a source and quote, or a quote joined with `...`. Fix them
  and post it again.
