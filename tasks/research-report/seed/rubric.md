# Rubric

`checks/grade.py` scores each criterion from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) against the
goal in README.md. Changing this file needs the administrator or the product owner.

- **coverage**: the report answers every question the goal asks, and every section `outline.md` plans is there.
- **support**: each figure is backed by the source its footnote cites, the quotes say what the text claims, and calculations are right.
- **sources**: primary and current sources are preferred, and the date or age of each figure is clear.
- **conflicts**: where sources disagree, the report shows and explains it instead of averaging it away.
- **usefulness**: the summary gives its reader a clear answer and the trade-offs behind it.
